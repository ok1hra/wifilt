CALIBRATE ON THE WSPR PAGE ↗

Set the band and the power you want to measure first — a calibration describes the radio as it stands, and it is filed under that band and that power.

A whole matrix at once: CAL PLAN in the bar at the top of the JS8Call or WSPR page. It tunes the radio and sets the power itself, and it asks about the antenna before every band.

After reboot the interface scans and starts with whichever configured network it can see, strongest first — not always profile 1. If that one refuses it alternates to the other; after four failed attempts, or when neither network is on the air, it falls back to AP mode. AP mode is also reachable from the USB-C serial console.
